quarter or sub-UNORM8 FLOAT coverage. Actual source UV1, nonidentity affine/typed

must retain exact complete source/typed/hidden owners and radiance. The hidden
source is never replaced by the ordinary comparison control.

channel adapter refusal remains a failure to diagnose, rather than a skipped case

guards remain; these four cases do not claim their full coordinate/frame transport.
