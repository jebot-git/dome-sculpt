completion of the authoring goal. Frozen components and their evidence remain
separate; a successful build will not establish cross-feature correctness.

## Source25 compatibility review

Docs' latest qualified source-rest checkpoint is `2192365`, based on `2f06246`
and `bbb1792`. It is intentionally not merged into this first source union.
Its exact pose-local matrices require conditional Deformation2 and Scene25;
`rig_source` capsules on both current objects and retained PolygonOrigin require
polygon_sources6 and recursive feature/budget/capture traversal. A future union
must include these roots inside color preparation, color UV, writable paint,
normal preparation, normal UV and normal sampling before-deformations, while
also retaining Source26 raw-model/placement closure. Scene26 must not implicitly
admit unproven Scene25 roots or reinterpret source-local frames as world data.
Build-only compatibility is insufficient; actual rig/reflected placement,
Save/reopen/restore/export consumers remain necessary.

No source-rest or Scene25 support is claimed by this branch. Full raw painting,
extra-UV transport through arbitrary topology, generic FBX UV2+, compact-layout
material compilation, and subsequent controller drawers remain required work.
