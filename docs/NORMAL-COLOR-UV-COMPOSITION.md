## Implemented contracts

A genuine native Bake can explicitly prepare current-corner colour and normal
attributes in the same detached transaction, before any historical baked-normal
bytes exist. Colour retains its actual stable-corner basis, immutable initial
receipt and exact before-paint/material. Normal preparation independently owns
its target frames, initial planes and complete before-state. Neither receipt
provides authority for the other field.

The paired UV path requires explicit Keep-detail normal sampling. Current RGBA
is independently projected and measured against its owning corner identities;
normal coordinates and captured normal-specific detail identity are retained by
the normal21 replay. The current colour descriptor and normal sampling graph
share the actual prior paint/material owners. Material8 conditionally carries
colour-entry3 and normal-entry3 contexts. Colour-only Scene23 emits no empty
normal pool; normal-only Scene21/material7 and old Scene20/material6 remain
conditional. Both pools, before-state aliases and recursive feature/budget edges
are preserved in a combined Scene23 save.

Bake readiness only admits initial version1 paired normal attributes for a
current-corner target. Retained version2 rebaking, UV1, typed MToon and unsupported
artist-role combinations retain their guards. This does not complete those
remaining requirements or general topology transfer.
