the separate simultaneous-source/target fixture `8496dec`. It changes no product
source, public layout, permissions, sampler policy, numerical tolerance or codec.

project rather than inferring composition from separate feature tests.

The fixture imports the bundled genuine CC0 female VRM with three explicitly
authored rigid accessory nodes added to its nineteen original skin parts. All
twenty-two parts share the exact raw original owner. It selects the real face
skin through the imported asset material table, checks its original normal image

UV moves use actual Pick and Apply without relaxing overlap or quality checks.

validated RigEdit applies the key and a linked Head pose, including the actual
rigid descendant. Replacing an existing original artist slot is covered by the
separate post-UV avatar fixture, not inferred as part of this combined fixture.

complete project. Retry prepares the current target and bakes the current source

source, material, deformation, shape and affine owners and world matrices remain
exact. PaintedMesh snapshot containers intentionally differ; their immutable
mesh owners and complete authored values, rather than container addresses, are
